Privacy Policy

1. Local Browser Storage

This pure HTML edition runs entirely in your browser without a remote server database. Demo preferences (theme choice, wallet balance, orders, and generated local ID) are kept in localStorage and IndexedDB on your own device.

2. Clearing Your Data

You can clear all stored demo records anytime from the Menu page by clicking Reset All Demo Data, or by clearing your browser site data.

3. Contact

For questions about customizing or deploying this template, replace support@boosthub.example with your own support address.
